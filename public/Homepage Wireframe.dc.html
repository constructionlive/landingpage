With Construction Life:
✓One paper trail — emails, logs, meetings, site events

General Contractor
Every RFI, invoice and change order — reconciled, not just received.
RFIs, invoices and bids pour in from subs. An invoice that doesn't match its RFI, a change order that doesn't match the drawing — easy to miss, flagged automatically.
With Construction Life:
✓Invoice ↔ RFI mismatches flagged automatically
✓Change order ↔ drawing update mismatches caught
✓Bid leveling across subcontractors
✓Emails auto-linked to schedule, submittal, RFI, budget
Project Manager

With Construction Life:
✓Daily logs → reports, generated & sent automatically

✓AI email tracking so nothing slips through

Time & material tracking, from a 40-hour-a-month chore to a photo.

Now: Crews snap a photo in the app. It's priced against a live database, reports generate on demand, and submission, approval, and tracking all flow through the system.
